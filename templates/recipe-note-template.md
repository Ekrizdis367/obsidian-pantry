## Ingredients

- 1 lb bone-in chicken thighs
- 2 cups crushed tomatoes
- 3 cloves garlic

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken and sear until golden.
2. Simmer with tomatoes until the chicken reaches 165°F.
3. Rest briefly, then serve over rice.
